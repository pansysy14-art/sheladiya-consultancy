## 📂 Homepage Sections

1. **Premium Navigation Bar**: Fixed frosted navy blur header, live section spy, quick telephone & email placeholders, consultation modal trigger, and mobile drawer.
2. **Hero Section**: Majestic monogram insignia, company headline, official tagline badge, 3 foundational pillars (Truth, Honesty, Discipline), and dual CTA actions.
3. **About Sheladiya Consultancy**: Founding principles, institutional profile, clear separation of regulated legal practice vs. corporate advisory, and editable establishment details.
4. **Legal Services (Regulated Advocate Practice)**: Civil & commercial disputes, corporate litigation/arbitration, statutory notices, IP/trademark protection, and employment law with Bar Council compliance notice.
5. **Corporate Consultancy**: Corporate governance, company incorporation, MCA/RoC secretarial compliance, commercial licensing, M&A restructuring, and FEMA/FDI advisory.
6. **Business Documentation**: High-stakes contracts (MSAs, SLAs, NDAs, Shareholder agreements, Employment pacts, and Contract Redlining / Risk Audit).
7. **Property Consultancy**: 30-Year Title Search Due Diligence, Sub-Registrar chain scrutiny, Conveyance deeds, Commercial leases, Land NA conversion, and RERA compliance.
8. **Professional Team**: Structured advisory profile slots with strictly designated editable placeholders (`[Senior Partner / Advocate Name]`, `[Managing Consultant / Partner Name]`, etc.). *No invented advocate names or fake qualifications.*
9. **Contact Information & Booking**: Chambers address placeholder, phone & email channels, operating hours, and an interactive Consultation Request Form with voluntary initiation confirmation.
10. **Premium Footer**: SC Monogram branding, practice index, back-to-top action, and Bar Council Rule 36 compliant regulatory disclaimer.

---

## ⚖️ Professional Compliance Standards

- **Zero Fake Claims:** No invented advocate names, fabricated testimonials, or artificial case success rates.
- **Editable Placeholders:** Missing company details are cleanly tagged with `[Editable Placeholders]` for simple client personalization.
- **Bar Council Rule 36 Compliance:** Information provided strictly for voluntary visitor facilitation without prohibited advertisement or solicitation.
- **Division Demarcation:** Regulated court advocacy (Advocates) is cleanly distinguished from corporate, documentation, and property advisory.
